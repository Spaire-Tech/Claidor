Questions.

What is Simeon?

A team of AI agents for people who run a business. Each one has a job, like your inbox, your money or your sales, a computer of its own in the cloud, and access to the tools you already use. You tell them what you need and they do the work.

Do I have to set anything up?

No workflows to build. Ask for what you need in plain words. When an agent needs one of your apps, it shows you a card, and you sign in on that app’s own page.

Where do the agents work?

On computers of their own in the cloud, each with a browser and files. You talk to them from the Simeon app on your Mac, and routines keep running when your Mac is closed.

Will they do anything without asking?

Not anything that matters. Before an agent sends an email, makes a payment or publishes something, it asks, and you allow or refuse it in the chat.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon. Every plan starts with a free week.
