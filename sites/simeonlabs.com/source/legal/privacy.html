The short version

  • We use your information to run Simeon for you, and for nothing else you would not expect.
  • We never use your content to train AI models, and we do not let the AI providers we use train on it.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Your agents only reach the apps you connect, within the permissions you grant. You can disconnect any of them at any time.
  • Each agent works on its own cloud computer, kept apart from every other customer’s.
  • You can see, export or delete your information, and you can delete your account at any time.

This summary is here to help you read what follows. The policy below is what applies.

Who we are and what this covers

Simeon is made by Simeon Labs, Inc. (“Simeon Labs”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers the personal information we handle when you use Simeon’s apps for Mac and iPhone, the agents you create and the cloud computers they work on, our website at simeonlabs.com and the web app at app.simeonlabs.com (together, the “Services”). For this information, Simeon Labs is the “controller”, the company responsible for it.

This policy does not cover the apps and services you connect to Simeon, such as your email or calendar provider. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with your information.

What we collect

What you give us

  • Account information. Your name, email address and profile picture, and how you sign in.
  • Your content. What you send your agents and what they send back: messages, voice, files, pictures, instructions, routines, and the names, roles and settings you give your agents.
  • Memory. What your agents remember about you and your work so they do not need to ask again, such as your preferences and the people you work with. You can see and change it in the app.
  • Payment information. Your plan and billing history. Card details are collected and kept by our payment processor; we do not store your full card number.
  • Messages to us. What you write when you contact support or send us feedback.

What your agents handle for you

  • Connected apps. When you connect an app, we receive an access credential for it, and your agents read and write what a task needs there: for example emails, events, documents, contacts or records. We access only what you allowed on the app’s own sign-in page.
  • Agents’ computers. Each agent’s cloud computer holds the files it works on, its browser history and downloads, and the websites it or you have signed in to on it. Your agents can take screenshots of their own screens to see what they are doing.
  • Voice calls. When you call an agent, your voice is streamed to turn it into text and the agent’s reply is turned into speech. We keep the call’s transcript in your chat; we do not keep a recording of the audio.
  • Channels. If you connect a channel such as Discord or Slack, we receive the messages sent to your agents there.

What we collect automatically

  • Device and usage information. Your app version, device type and operating system, IP address, the time of your requests, how much your agents work and on what model, and error reports.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the token Apple gives us to send them to your device.
  • Cookies. The web app uses cookies to keep you signed in. See Cookies and the website.

What we receive from others

If you sign in with another service, such as Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and picture from it. Connected apps send us the information described above.

How we use it

  • To run Simeon for you: to create and run your agents, carry out the tasks and routines you give them, operate their cloud computers, connect your apps, keep your chats and memory, and send you notifications.
  • To keep it safe: to authenticate you, protect accounts, detect and stop abuse, fraud and security incidents, and enforce our Terms.
  • To support you: to answer your questions and fix problems, which may include looking at the part of your account that a problem concerns.
  • To bill you: to manage your plan, credits and payments.
  • To improve Simeon: to understand reliability, speed, cost and which features are used, mostly from usage counts and error reports.
  • To talk to you: to send you service messages, such as about your account, security or changes to our terms, and, if you agree, news about Simeon, which you can turn off at any time.
  • To meet legal duties: to comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your content to build profiles about you for anyone else.

AI models and training

Your agents think with large language models made by other companies. To answer you and do a task, we send the relevant part of your content to a model provider, which processes it to produce a response.

We never use your content to train AI models, and the model providers we use do not train on it. They process it under terms that forbid training on it, and may keep it for a limited time, usually no more than 30 days, only to detect and prevent abuse, unless the law requires otherwise.

Connected apps

You choose which apps to connect, and you grant access on each app’s own sign-in page. Your agents use that access only for the tasks you give them. You can disconnect an app at any time in Simeon’s settings, and you can also revoke Simeon’s access in the app itself. When you disconnect, we delete the credential we held for it.

Simeon’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we use data from Google services only to provide the features you use in Simeon; we do not use it for advertising; we do not use it to train AI models; we do not sell it; and no person at Simeon Labs reads it unless you ask us to (for example for support), it is needed for security or to comply with the law, or it has been aggregated and anonymised.

For legal reasons

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or that it is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or Simeon Labs. Where we can, we will tell you before we disclose your information in response to a legal demand.

If our business changes hands

If Simeon Labs is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of it, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

How we protect it

We encrypt your information in transit and at rest, keep each customer’s agents and their cloud computers apart from every other customer’s, limit our staff’s access to what their work requires, and keep the credentials of your connected apps on our servers, never on other customers’ computers.

No system is perfectly secure, and AI agents bring risks of their own: a website, email or document an agent reads can contain instructions meant to mislead it. Asking before important actions is one of our protections against that. Review what an agent asks to do, and tell us at security@simeonlabs.com if you find a security problem.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your account information, chats, memory, files and agents’ computers so your agents can keep working, unless you delete them sooner.
  • When you delete something, such as a chat, an agent or a connected app, we remove it from the Services. Copies in backups are deleted within 35 days.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your information and your agents’ computers within 30 days, and from backups within a further 35 days.
  • Logs and error reports are kept for up to 90 days, unless we need them longer to investigate a security incident.
  • Billing records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 7 years.

We may keep information longer when the law requires it, to resolve disputes, or to enforce our agreements.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change. You can see and edit your account, your agents, their memory and your chats in the app.
  • Disconnect. You can disconnect any app or channel at any time.
  • Delete. You can delete chats, agents and your whole account in the app’s settings, or by emailing us.
  • Get a copy. You can ask us for a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Emails. You can unsubscribe from news about Simeon with the link in each email. We will still send you service messages.
  • Notifications and microphone. You can turn them off in your device’s settings.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent you gave. To use any of these rights, email privacy@simeonlabs.com. We will confirm your identity, usually from the email of your account, and answer within the time the law sets, normally within 30 days. You may also have someone you authorise make a request for you. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.
