# Use cases

Simeon works best when each agent has one clear job. The [Marketplace](/marketplace) has ready-made teams of five agents for common kinds of work; add one to Simeon in a click, then rename the agents or change their jobs.

## First tasks to try

Each of these is a good first message. Change the details to fit your work.

### Inbox

> Every morning, sort my inbox. Reply to anything that only needs a yes or a date, flag what needs me, and give me a short summary at 9.

### Finance

> Match this month's transactions in QuickBooks, chase any invoice more than 14 days late with a friendly reminder, and tell me where the money went.

### Travel

> Find me the cheapest refundable flight from Seattle to LA tomorrow, landing before 2pm. Show me the options before booking.

### Social media

> Turn what we shipped this week into three posts for Instagram and LinkedIn. Show me the drafts and schedule the ones I approve.

### Research

> Find the ten companies most likely to need what we sell, with the right person to contact at each and why.

## Tips for a good brief

- **One job per agent.** "Inbox" and "Finance" work better as two agents than one "assistant".
- **Say where to stop.** Tell the agent what it may do alone and what it should show you first.
- **Teach it once.** Correct the first results and ask the agent to remember how you like things done.
