# FAQ

## Simeon

### What is Simeon?

A team of AI agents for your business. Each agent has a job you give it, a computer of its own in the cloud, and access to the apps you connect. You ask in plain words, and they do the work. See [Create and manage agents](/docs/agents).

### Do I need to set anything up?

No. There are no workflows to build. Tell an agent what you need. When it needs one of your apps, it asks you to sign in once, on that app's own page.

### What do I need to use it?

A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 15 or later, or an iPhone on iOS 26 or later. The same account works on both. See [Get started](/docs/get-started) and [Simeon on iPhone](/docs/iphone).

## Agents and their work

### How many agents can I have?

Up to 50, on every plan. Your plan sets how much they can do each week, not how many there are.

### Where does the work happen?

On each agent's own desktop on your team's cloud computer, with a browser and files. You can watch it work or take control at any time. Routines keep running while your Mac is closed. See [The agent's computer](/docs/computer).

### Can my agents use apps that don't have a connector?

Yes. They can use most websites in their browser, signed in as you. A connector is faster and more reliable when there is one. See [Connect apps](/docs/connectors).

### Can I talk to an agent?

Yes. Call any agent from its chat and talk it through out loud. See [Message and call your agents](/docs/messaging).

### Can my agents work with Claude Code, Codex or ChatGPT?

Yes. Connect them in **External Agents** and they join your Simeon Network: they can ask your Simeon agents for work, and your agents can ask them. See [Simeon Network](/docs/network).

## Approvals and privacy

### Will an agent act without asking?

Not on what matters. An email or a Slack message in your name comes as a draft you send yourself, and a purchase waits on a checkout card until you choose **Allow**. See [Approvals, security, and privacy](/docs/approvals).

### Can an agent spend my money?

Only through Link or Shop Pay, once you've added one in **Settings › Wallet**, and only after you allow the checkout card, confirmed with Face ID or Touch ID. Each Link purchase uses a one-time card, and the agent never sees a card number.

### Do agents see my passwords?

A password you type in an agent's sign-in form goes straight into the page, and the agent doesn't see it. Logins you save in the Credentials Store are there for your agents to use. See [Credentials Store and secure sign-in](/docs/credentials).

### Is my data used to train AI?

No. We never use your content to train AI models, and the AI providers we use work under terms that don't let them train on it. Your data is encrypted in transit and kept apart from every other customer's. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Plans and billing

### How much does Simeon cost?

Standard is $30 a month, Pro $100 (3.5 times Standard's usage) and Max $200 (9 times). Every plan has every feature; they differ in how much your agents can do each week. See [Plans and billing](/docs/billing).

### Is there a free trial?

Yes, 7 days with one week of Standard credits. On iPhone it starts when you first sign in, with no card. On the web and the Mac it starts with a card, and becomes your plan when the 7 days end unless you cancel it.

### What happens when my credits run out?

Your agents wait until your week resets. To keep going sooner, upgrade your plan or buy extra credits: $10 for 3,000,000 credits, valid for 12 months.

### Can I use my own ChatGPT or Claude plan?

Yes. Connect it in **Settings › AI Models** and choose its model. Your agents' work on it uses no Simeon credits; you still need a Simeon plan for their computers.

### How do I cancel?

For a plan bought on the web or the Mac, choose **Manage Billing** in **Settings › Usage & Billing** and cancel on Stripe's page. For a plan bought on iPhone, open the iPhone's **Settings › your name › Subscriptions**.

## Your account

### How do I delete my account?

In the Simeon app on your Mac or iPhone, open **Settings** and choose **Delete Account**. Your agents, their computers and files, your chats, memory and routines are deleted, your apps disconnected and your subscription ended. It can't be undone. A plan billed through Apple isn't cancelled by this: cancel it in the iPhone's **Settings › your name › Subscriptions**.

### Something isn't working

Quit and reopen the app first. If that doesn't fix it, choose **Report an issue** in the account menu, or [contact us](/contact) and tell us what you were doing.
