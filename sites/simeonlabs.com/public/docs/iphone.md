# Simeon for iPhone

Simeon for iPhone connects to the same agents, chats and computers as the Mac app. Sign in with the same account and everything is there.

> Simeon for iPhone isn't in the App Store yet. When it is, you'll find the download on [simeonlabs.com](/).

## What you can do on iPhone

- **Message your agents.** Type or dictate, attach photos, files and short videos.
- **Call an agent.** Talk to it out loud; it works in the background while the call goes on. See [Message and call your agents](/docs/messaging).
- **Approve or refuse.** Approval cards show up in the chat and as notifications, so an agent is never stuck waiting for your desk.
- **Watch the computer and take control.** Open an agent's computer to see its screen. **Take control** stops the agent at once and hands you the screen; give it back when you're done. See [The agent's computer](/docs/computer).
- **Connect apps.** The same **Connect** button as on the Mac.

## Requirements

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later.

## Plans on iPhone

On iPhone, plans are bought through the App Store, with a 7-day free trial that needs no card. You manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings. See [Plans and billing](/docs/billing).
