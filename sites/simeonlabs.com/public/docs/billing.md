# Plans and billing

Every plan gives you the whole product: your agents, their computers, connectors, routines and voice calls. Plans differ in how much your agents can do each week.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Usage each week |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | $30 a month | The base allowance |
| **Pro** | $100 a month | 3.5 times Standard |
| **Max** | $200 a month | 9 times Standard, and earliest access to new products |

On the web, each plan is also sold yearly at 20% off. See [Pricing](/pricing).

## Free trial

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. On the web it starts with a card and becomes your plan when the week ends, unless you cancel. On iPhone it needs no card. A trial includes one week of Standard usage, whichever plan you chose.

## Usage

Your agents' work uses credits, and each plan gives you a number of credits a week. The week resets on its own. If you run out before it resets, you can wait, move to a bigger plan, or buy extra credits.

## Extra credits

On a paid plan you can buy extra credits on the web: $10 for a pack. Extra credits are used only after the week's credits run out, and they last 12 months.

## Change your plan

- **Upgrade:** starts today. You pay the new plan, less what's left of your current one, and get a fresh week.
- **Downgrade:** takes effect at your next renewal; you keep your current plan until then.

## Cancel

On the web, manage or cancel your subscription from your account's billing page. On iPhone, plans are bought through the App Store, so manage or cancel them in your Apple ID settings.
