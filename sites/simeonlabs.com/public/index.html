Chief of Staff

Your Chief of Staff.
Runs the team for you.

Simeon sets the week’s priorities, hands each job to the right agent and tells you only what needs you. Ask it anything, in plain words.

Works in the tools you already use.

  • Just ask Simeon. Ask where a launch stands and it checks Linear, asks the team and comes back with the review doc.

  • Group chats with your team. Put Simeon and the agents who know the work in one chat. They answer each other, and you step in when you want.

  • Launches, kept on track. Yodo posts the standup in Slack every morning and flags anything stuck for more than a day.

Agents

An agent for any job.
Named like a person.

Describe the job in plain words and Simeon staffs an agent for it. Each one has its own computer in the cloud and works in your apps, the way a person on your team would.

Every agent asks before anything important.

  • Inbox and calendar. Mila answers what she can, files the rest and drafts the replies that need you. Change anything, then send.

  • Customer support. Iris answers tickets from your help docs in Notion and leaves refunds and unhappy customers for you.

  • Bookkeeping. Theo closes the month from Stripe and QuickBooks, tells you the runway and chases what’s late.

  • Customer research. Scout reads your interview notes and support conversations and brings back what customers keep saying.

And so much more.

  • Call any agent. Ask how things are going from your Mac. It answers with your real numbers, and what it did shows up in the chat.
  • Routines. Ask for a check-in every Monday and it happens every Monday, whether your Mac is open or not.
  • A computer of its own. Each agent works on its own computer in the cloud, with a browser and files. You can watch it, or take over.
  • Files you can open. Reports, sheets and docs land in the chat as real files, ready to download.
  • Connect apps with a card. When an agent needs one of your apps, it shows a card and you sign in on that app’s own page.
  • It learns how you work. Tell it once that mornings are for deep work, and it keeps them free.
