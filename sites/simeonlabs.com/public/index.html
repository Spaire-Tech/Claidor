Questions and answers

The short version of what people ask before they start.

What is Simeon?

A team of AI agents for your business. Each agent has a job you give it, a computer of its own in the cloud, and access to the apps you already use. You ask in plain words, and they do the work.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. There are no workflows to build. Tell an agent what you need. When it needs one of your apps, it asks you to sign in once, on that app’s own page.

Where does the work happen?

On each agent’s own computer in the cloud, with a browser and files. You can watch it work or take over at any time, and routines keep running when your Mac is closed.

Will an agent act without asking?

Not on anything that matters. Before it sends an email, makes a payment or publishes something, it asks you in the chat, and you allow or refuse it.

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted, kept apart from every other customer, and never used to train a model.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon. Every plan starts with a free week.
