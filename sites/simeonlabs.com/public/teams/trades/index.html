Simeon for Contractors

Five agents for running a trade business: quotes, the schedule, supplies, invoices and reviews.

Add to Simeon

  • Wes Quotes

    Turns calls into jobs. Prices the work from the photos and your rates, and follows up until the customer says yes.

    “The Patel bathroom quote is ready: $8,640 with materials, photos attached. Send it through Jobber?”

  • Rita Schedule

    Runs the day. Plans who goes where and the shortest route between jobs, and keeps the crew in the loop.

    “Tomorrow: 7 jobs, 2 crews, and the routes save 50 minutes of driving. Send the plan to the crews?”

  • Hal Supplies

    Orders the materials. Gets the parts on site before the job starts and keeps spending in check.

    “The Elm St job needs 14 sheets of drywall. Best price is the yard on 3rd at $212. Order it?”

  • Joan Invoices

    Gets you paid. Invoices the day a job ends, takes card payments on site and chases what is late.

    “4 jobs finished this week, $11,250 to invoice. The Diaz invoice is 21 days late. Send them and a reminder?”

  • Max Reviews

    Builds your name. Asks happy customers for reviews, answers every one and shows off the finished work.

    “Six jobs finished this week. Review requests are ready, and two before-and-after posts. Send and post?”
