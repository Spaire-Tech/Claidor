Five agents for selling: research, outreach, the CRM, coaching and the pipeline.

  • Ivy Research

    Knows the account. Finds the right companies and people, and has a one-page brief ready before every call.

    “Kestrel raised $30M on Tuesday and is hiring 6 sales reps. Their VP of Sales came from a customer. Add them?”

  • Omar Outreach

    Starts conversations. Writes outreach that sounds like you, by email and on LinkedIn, and follows up until someone answers.

    “The sequence for 40 fintech CFOs is ready: 3 emails, 2 LinkedIn touches. Last one got 11% replies. Launch it?”

  • Greta CRM

    Keeps the CRM clean. Logs every call and next step so you never update a field by hand again.

    “Logged 6 calls from today. Brightline moved to Proposal at $48k, closing Nov 14. Look right?”

  • Rory Coaching

    Makes every call better. Reviews your calls, spots what wins deals and gives you one thing to try next time.

    “You talked 68% of the time on the Arbor call and skipped pricing. Want one tip for Thursday’s follow-up?”

  • Petra Pipeline

    Calls the number. Tracks every deal against quota, flags the ones at risk and has the forecast ready.

    “You’re at 71% of quota with 3 weeks left. Two deals worth $60k have no next step. Want a plan?”
