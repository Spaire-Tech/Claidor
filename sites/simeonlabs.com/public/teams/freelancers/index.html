Simeon for Freelancers

Five agents for working on your own: inbox, proposals, invoices, time and leads.

Add to Simeon

  • Elsa Inbox

    Clears the inbox. Sorts every client message, drafts the replies and turns each request into a task.

    “Twelve new emails, two need you today: Dana’s revisions and a new inquiry. Replies are drafted. Send them?”

  • Marco Proposals

    Wins the work. Turns a discovery call into a clear proposal with the scope, the timeline and the price.

    “The Juniper rebrand proposal is ready: 6 weeks, $9,800, two rounds of revisions. Send it?”

  • Talia Invoices

    Gets you paid. Sends the invoices on time, chases the late ones and knows what is still owed.

    “Hollis Studio is 18 days late on $2,400. A friendly reminder is drafted. Send it?”

    Protects your time. Books calls into the right slots, tracks the hours and warns when a project runs over.

    “You’ve logged 31 hours on Fernway against 24 quoted. Flag it to them before the next phase?”

  • Lucia Leads

    Finds the next client. Spots good jobs and warm leads, drafts the pitch and keeps the pipeline in one place.

    “Three Upwork jobs fit your work, one at $85 an hour from a past client. The pitches are drafted. Want to see them?”
