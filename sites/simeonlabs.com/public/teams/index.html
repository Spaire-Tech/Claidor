Questions and answers

How do I add a team?

Open it here and choose Add to Simeon on the Mac or iPhone where Simeon is installed. The app opens and adds all five agents to a folder in your sidebar.

Does a team cost extra?

No. Adding one is free, and its agents use your plan like any agent you create. A team that works every day may suit Pro.

What if I already use some of their apps?

Each agent names the apps you have already connected and asks only for the ones it is missing.

Can I change them once they are added?

Yes, they are yours. Rename them, rewrite what they do, give them other apps, or remove the ones you don’t need. The rest keep working.

Can I add more than one team?

Yes. Each goes in its own folder. Adding a team you already have simply opens it.

What if my kind of work isn’t here?

Start from the closest team and reshape it, or create your own agents from scratch in the app.
