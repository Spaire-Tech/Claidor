  • Lola Bookings

    Fills the book. Turns DMs into bookings, fits them into the right chair and keeps the gaps to a minimum.

    “Two DMs asking for balayage on Saturday. Ana has 10am and 2pm free. Hold both?”

  • Remi Reminders

    Stops no-shows. Reminds every client before the visit, checks who is coming and refills the empty slots.

    “14 clients tomorrow, 12 confirmed, two haven’t answered. Offer the 3pm to the waitlist?”

  • Nell Reviews

    Keeps the stars up. Asks happy clients for a review and answers every one, good or bad.

    “Four new 5-star reviews this week and one 2-star about the wait. Replies are drafted. Post them?”

  • Kira Socials

    Shows off the work. Turns before-and-afters into videos, posts and pins that bring in new clients.

    “The copper balayage clip is ready for TikTok, plus 3 pins for Pinterest. Post them at 7pm tonight?”

  • Dora Win-back

    Brings clients back. Spots who hasn’t visited in a while, asks why and sends an offer worth coming back for.

    “41 clients haven’t booked in 3 months. A 'we miss you' email with 15% off is ready. Send it?”
