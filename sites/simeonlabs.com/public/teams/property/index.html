Simeon for Landlords

Five agents for renting out homes: tenants, repairs, rent, leases and vacancies.

Add to Simeon

  • Dalia Tenants

    Answers every tenant. Replies fast, logs every request and keeps each tenant’s file up to date.

    “Unit 3B says the heat is off. Logged as urgent, and a reply is drafted. Send it?”

  • Bram Repairs

    Gets things fixed. Books the right contractor, lets them in and checks the job is done.

    “The plumber can fix 12 Oak St on Thursday at 9. A door code for 9 to 11 is ready. Confirm?”

    Collects the rent. Tracks who paid, chases who didn’t and has the numbers for each property.

    “Rent is in for 11 of 12 units. 5A is 4 days late, the first time this year. Send a friendly reminder?”

    Handles the leases. Drafts each one, gets it signed and reminds you well before it ends.

    “Three leases end in January. Renewal offers at 3% are drafted. Send them for signature?”

  • Tess Vacancies

    Fills empty units. Writes the listing, finds good tenants and books the viewings.

    “2B’s listing got 23 inquiries and eight look qualified. Viewings can go Saturday 10 to 12. Book them?”
