Asleep when idle.
Backed up every two minutes.

After 30 minutes without work, the computer sleeps, with your files kept. A message or a routine wakes it. A message sent while it sleeps still reaches your agent.

Your files, your agents' work and their sign-ins are copied every two minutes, and after each task. A new computer starts from that copy.

Questions and answers

Does each agent have its own computer?

Each person has one computer in the cloud. Every agent works on a desktop of its own on it, with its own Chrome.

Can I use it myself?

Yes. Open it from an agent’s chat on your Mac or iPhone, and take control.

Where do my files go?

Files you send to an agent land on the computer. Files your agents make come back to you in the chat.

How big is it?

4 GB of memory and 2 CPUs.

What isn’t backed up?

Your Claude sign-in. It stays on the computer only.
