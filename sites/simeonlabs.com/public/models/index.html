Questions and answers

Subscription or API key?

A subscription is a monthly plan you already have, such as ChatGPT Plus. Your agents use it within its limits. An API key is billed by its provider for each call, at its own prices.

Where do I connect one?

In Simeon, open Settings › AI Models. Plans are under Subscriptions, keys under API keys.

How do I choose the model?

In the message field. You can go back to Simeon’s models anytime.

What happens when my plan runs out?

Your agents use Simeon’s models until it resets.

What still uses my Simeon credits?

Voice calls, dictation and video.

Where is my key kept?

Encrypted on Simeon’s server, and sent only to its provider. Your agents never see it.

How do I disconnect?

In Settings › AI Models. Your agents stop using that account, and you can connect it again anytime.

What does Coming soon mean?

GitHub Copilot and Meta can’t be connected yet.
