Questions and answers

How does the free month work?

Choose a plan and add a card. The first 30 days are free, then the plan’s monthly price begins. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. There is one free month per person.

What is usage?

The work your agents do for you. Each plan includes an amount for the week, which resets every Monday and doesn’t carry over. Pro includes 3.5 times Standard’s, and Max 12 times.

What happens if I use it all?

Your agents wait until Monday, when it resets. The app tells you as you get close, and you can move to a bigger plan at any time.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time, from Settings in the app or from your billing page. Cancel during the free month and you are never charged.

Do the computers, apps or teams cost extra?

No. Each agent’s computer in the cloud, every app you connect and every team you add from the Marketplace come with every plan, the free month too. Their work counts toward your plan’s usage.

How do I pay?

By card, through Stripe, in US dollars, billed monthly.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 15 or later.
