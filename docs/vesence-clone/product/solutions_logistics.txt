Quotes, Written for You 
Vesence reads the enquiry in your inbox, prices it against the rate cards and customer terms in SharePoint, and returns a finished quote in your own Word or Excel template. What was a manual job every morning becomes a review.

Environmental Reporting 
Hand over an environmental report and get each site's Naturvårdsverket classification back in the app — the activity code, whether it is notifiable or permit-required, and the passage in the report it came from. 
Miljorapport_2025.pdf 
PDF 
Read the environmental report and classify each site per Naturvårdsverket 
 Used 4 tools 
 Read Miljorapport_2025.pdf — 64 pages 
 Extracted 3 sites, 11 reported activities 
 Matched Miljöprövningsförordningen (2013:251) 
 Site Activity Code Status Warehouse, Gothenburg Fuel storage > 50 m³ C 39.30 Notifiable Terminal, Malmö Vehicle washing facility C 50.20 Notifiable Workshop, Jönköping Waste oil handling B 90.30 Permit required 
Jönköping is the one to look at: waste oil handling puts it in category B, which needs a permit rather than a notification. The other two stay notifiable at their current volumes.
