# Simeon — your AI operations team

**Simeon is a small team of AI assistants that runs the operations of your business. Each one has a job, its own computer in the cloud, and access to the tools you already use. You tell them what you need the way you'd tell staff. They do the work inside your tools and come back with the result, day and night.**

---

## The problem

Running a small company means doing two jobs. There's the work that grows the business, and there's the work that keeps it running: answering email, chasing invoices, updating the CRM, posting on LinkedIn, pulling numbers for the Monday meeting, following up after calls.

That second job is spread across ten apps that don't talk to each other. You copy from Stripe to a spreadsheet, from Gmail to Notion, from your calendar to a follow-up email. None of it is hard. All of it takes hours, every week, and it never ends.

Hiring an operations person costs $5,000+ a month. Chatbots can talk, but they can't *do* anything in your tools. Automation apps can, but only after you build and maintain every workflow yourself.

## What Simeon is

Simeon gives you a team instead of a tool.

- **A team, not one bot.** Each assistant has a role: Chief of Staff, Inbox, Money, Growth, Content, Sales. They stay focused on their job and hand work to each other.
- **They work inside your tools.** Gmail, Notion, Stripe, Calendar, Slack, HubSpot, Canva, LinkedIn and 60+ others. You connect each one once, in a tap.
- **Each one has its own computer in the cloud.** They keep working when your laptop is closed: overnight, on weekends, while you're in meetings.
- **You talk to them like staff.** No setup, no workflows to build. "Find this." "Fix that." "Tell me every Monday how we're doing."
- **They come back with the result,** not a to-do list for you.

## How it works

1. **Pick your team.** Simeon starts you with the roles a business like yours needs. Rename them, remove them, add more.
2. **Connect your tools.** When an assistant needs an app, it shows you a card. Tap Add, sign in, and it's connected. No passwords shared in chat.
3. **Ask for what you need.** In plain words, in chat or by voice.
4. **Let it run.** Turn anything you do every week into a routine: "Every Monday at 9, send me revenue, new customers and failed payments."

## A week with Simeon

**Monday, 9:00.** Your Money assistant posts last week's numbers: revenue up 12%, two failed payments, one invoice 30 days overdue.

**Monday, 9:01.** You reply: "Chase the overdue one." It asks your Inbox assistant to draft a polite follow-up. You read it, tap send.

**Tuesday.** You ask: "Who did I promise something to in last week's calls?" It goes through your calendar and notes and lists four follow-ups, with drafts ready.

**Wednesday.** "Turn the launch note in Notion into a LinkedIn post and a one-page graphic." Your Content assistant writes the post and makes the graphic in Canva. You approve both.

**Friday.** Your Chief of Staff sends the week in five lines: what moved, what's stuck, what needs you on Monday.

You didn't open Stripe, a spreadsheet, or Canva once.

## The team

Every business is different, so the team is yours to shape. A typical founder's team:

| Assistant | What it does | Works in |
|---|---|---|
| **Chief of Staff** | Runs the team. Weekly priorities, follow-ups, the big picture. | Notion, Calendar |
| **Inbox** | Sorts email, drafts replies, flags what needs you. Never sends without your OK. | Gmail, Outlook |
| **Money** | Revenue, runway, failed payments, overdue invoices. | Stripe, Mercury, QuickBooks |
| **Growth** | Traffic, signups, what's working and what isn't. | Google Analytics, PostHog |
| **Content** | Posts, newsletters, decks and graphics. | LinkedIn, Canva, Kit |
| **Sales** | Leads, follow-ups, a pipeline that doesn't go stale. | HubSpot, Attio, Calendly |

They talk to each other. Ask your Chief of Staff to "chase every overdue invoice", and it gets the list from Money, the drafts from Inbox, and brings you one message to approve.

## Why Simeon is different

**Compared with ChatGPT and other chatbots:** a chatbot answers questions. Simeon does the work, inside your real tools, and keeps doing it on a schedule without being asked again.

**Compared with Zapier and other automation apps:** with automation you build every workflow yourself and fix it when it breaks. With Simeon you just say what you want, and it works out the steps.

**Compared with hiring:** an operations hire costs thousands a month and works 40 hours a week. Simeon costs a fraction of that, works around the clock, and never forgets to follow up.

## You stay in control

- **Nothing important happens without your yes.** Sending email, moving money, publishing, deleting: the assistant asks first, with a clear Yes / No card.
- **You see everything.** Every action is in the conversation. Nothing happens in the dark.
- **Your accounts stay yours.** You sign in to each tool directly; Simeon never asks for your passwords in chat. Disconnect any tool at any time.
- **It only remembers what you told it.** Simeon keeps notes so you don't repeat yourself, but only facts you said or confirmed, never its own guesses.
