# The Swens AHA — the founder's research, the lead's verdict and dispositions

27 August 2026. The founder's research agent delivered a measured
report (its own mini-experiment: a 330-cell project-finance model,
six planted defects, answer key before detectors, static-vs-dynamic
table, and a self-correction that narrowed its own headline claim).
The lead's verdict: **this is the build's thesis crystallized** —
« thirty years of spreadsheet research, and every published tool
reads the file; we are the only one who can run it. » The
recalculator (B1/B2, 99.88% over 3.86M cells) is not plumbing; it is
the trace generator the entire invariant-discovery literature
assumed and no one in this industry has.

## How it lands on the plan

It converges with **B5 (approved yesterday)** and extends it. The
report's measured findings become B5's design laws; its product idea
becomes a Watch amendment; its cheap static check becomes an A-track
candidate. Nothing is a rewrite; everything sits on built parts.

### Adopted into orders now

1. **Sentinel — the reachability check** (« this assumption changes
   nothing in your model »): a dependency-graph walk, no
   recalculation, a defect class no shipped tool covers, a
   one-sentence banker finding. Through the normal loop: registered,
   planted, priced, gated. The report measured 23 of 35 dead cells
   found by graph alone on its toy — our number comes from our loop.
2. **Dynamo — B5 design laws** (from the report's own measured
   failures, adopted verbatim):
   - **Typed perturbation policy**: flags/selectors/toggles are held
     or stepped through real states, never scaled — the report mined
     a confident false rule from a model running in a mode it never
     occupies, and killed it by typing the inputs. The input-typing
     step is where the engineering lives.
   - **Fidelity-gate precondition**: never mine a file the gate
     refuses (standing rule, restated).
   - Non-converged runs are dropped, never data.
   - **PSLQ/LLL as the candidate mining engine** (integer-relation
     detection — accounting identities are ±1-coefficient
     cancellations, exactly PSLQ's problem; `mpmath`, one call)
     measured against the naive enumeration before adoption.
   - **Plant from reality**: B5's catch-rate defects come from the
     PR24 draft→final real diffs (84 genuine regressions nobody
     designed for us), not only from self-designed plants — the
     designer-knows-the-detector bias, named by the report.
   - The **Monday experiment** shape as B5's first registered round:
     3 gate-clean corpus models, inputs typed by hand (to price the
     typing), 200 runs each, mine signed-sum rules, **print the rule
     set and read it before scoring anything** — the test is whether
     a modeller recognises the model in its own discovered laws.
   - **Inert-reference check** joins B5's detectors (a named edge
     that carries nothing — graph sees it live, only running shows
     it dead).

### Proposed plan amendments (pending the founder's go)

- **C6 — the rule-set diff**: mine v8's laws, mine v12's laws, diff
  the law sets. « v12 obeys all 47 rules v8 obeyed, adds 2, broke
  1. » Survives inserted rows and renamed sheets by construction
  (behaviour, not positions); tier-2 strength, not proof, and said
  so. The report calls it its most valuable idea; the lead agrees —
  it is the Watch's product sentence.
- **Diagnosis layer (post-B5)**: a broken mined rule names four
  cells; Reiter's minimal-diagnosis (1987) + spectrum-based fault
  localisation compute the smallest culprit set — our
  one-authoring-decision-one-finding principle as an algorithm. The
  blocker that killed this line academically (spreadsheets have no
  failing tests) is removed by the mined rules themselves.
- **ddmin over version diffs** is already B4's amendment (24 Aug)
  and Dynamo's ordered round — the report independently converges.

### Filed, with the lead's reservations noted

- **VarMisuse/DeepBugs localisation** (D5-class: wrong-but-live
  reference — the one defect both checker families missed):
  localisation-only, never repair; self-supervised training by
  reference-swapping on our corpus. Filed as a future track — a
  learned model's finding must still be explained in words before it
  ships; that bar is high and stays.
- **Conformal FDR guarantee** (« flagged at 1% false discovery rate,
  with the mathematics »): commercially potent; the exchangeability
  caveat (calibrate per model family) is real. Filed for after the
  Tasi round teaches us how labels move across populations.
- **Sensitivity fingerprint** (sign checks, elasticity, the
  UNDERSTAND pillar's engine): same computation as B5's runs, sold
  twice. Filed to fold into B5's later rounds.
- Deep-weird list recorded as-is (interval arithmetic as the cheap
  tier under Z3; SEQUITUR irregularity; matrix-profile discords;
  Kirchhoff/null-space identities — the last is a candidate exact
  engine for B5 alongside PSLQ). BlueFin (arXiv 2605.30907): the
  30-point formula-vs-perturbation gap is outside evidence for the
  thesis; to cite when we publish.

## The lead's one caution

The report's numbers (4/6 dynamic, 0 false positives) are from its
author's own toy with its author's own plants — it says so itself.
Nothing from it enters our record as a result; what enters is the
design. Our numbers come from registered rounds on the real corpus,
as always.
