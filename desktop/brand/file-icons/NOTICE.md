# File icons

`word.svg` and `pdf.svg` are the artwork the Word add-in already ships
(`clients/apps/word-addin/src/ui/fileIconAssets.ts`); `excel.svg` and
`powerpoint.svg` are `file_type_excel.svg` and `file_type_powerpoint.svg`
from the same set, vscode-icons (https://github.com/vscode-icons/vscode-icons),
MIT License, Copyright (c) 2016 Roberto Huertas. The product logos they carry
are trademarks of Microsoft and Adobe, used to identify the file type.

`../app-logos/` holds the Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive logos from
the onboarding design (`docs/maties/design/onboarding/logos/`); they are
Google's trademarks, used to identify the service. `google-drive.svg` is
stored uncompressed here (the onboarding copy is gzip under an `.svg` name).
