# CCJA Règlements — acquisition notes

The Court's own instruments: how a pourvoi reaches the CCJA, the deadlines
before it, and how it administers arbitration. The corpus holds the
uniform acts and the Court's judgments but none of this, which is why a
question about CCJA procedure currently has no answer.

Retrieved 8 August 2026 from the official OHADA site (`ohada.org`), via
its WordPress media library.

## What is here

### `ohada-org-reglement-arbitrage-ccja.pdf` — usable

- Source: <https://www.ohada.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/reglement-arbitrage-ccja.pdf>
- 17 pages, ~41,500 characters, text layer intact (no OCR needed).
- Structure: `ART. 1` … `ART. 34`, chapter headings present.
- Preamble: « Vu le Traité …, notamment en ses articles 8 et 26 ; Vu le
  règlement de procédures de la Cour Commune de Justice et d'Arbitrage,
  notamment en son article 54 ; Vu l'avis en date du 9 décembre 1998 de
  la Cour Commune de Justice et d'arbitrage ».

**Version not established — do not load yet.** The document carries no
adoption date of its own. The single date it contains, 9 December 1998,
belongs to a CCJA *avis* the Conseil des ministres cites before adopting;
it is not the date of this text. The known candidates are the original
règlement (adopted 11 March 1999) and the revision of 23 November 2017,
and 34 articles with no mention of 2017 points to the original — but
"points to" is not a citation. Loading a text under the wrong date would
put the version gate itself in the wrong, which is the one failure this
product cannot afford.

To settle it, one of: the Journal Officiel de l'OHADA issue carrying the
adoption; the signature block on a JO-sourced copy; or the 2017 revised
text alongside it for comparison (the diff engine in `polar/analysis`
would then show which one this is).

### `ohada-org-REGLEMENT_INTEREIEUR_CCJA_EN_MATIERE_ARBITRAGE.pdf` — unusable as acquired

- Source: <https://www.ohada.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/REGLEMENT_INTEREIEUR_CCJA_EN_MATIERE_ARBITRAGE.pdf>
- 6 pages, **5 characters of extractable text**: a scan with no text
  layer. It needs OCR before it can be read, and is kept here as acquired
  so the provenance chain stays unbroken.

## Still missing

- **Règlement de procédure de la CCJA** (18 April 1996, amended 30
  January 2014) — the text every pourvoi and every demande d'avis runs
  on, and the one the avis above cite by article. Not present in the
  ohada.org media library under any search tried (`règlement`,
  `procédure`, `CCJA`); it is referenced by several pages of the site
  without a downloadable copy attached.

## Side finding

The preamble records a CCJA avis of **9 December 1998**. Juricaf's
collection does not hold it — the earliest avis there is 001/1999 of
7 July 1999. The advisory series is therefore larger than the five avis
now identified in the corpus.
