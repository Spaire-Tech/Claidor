SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made BETWEEN:

(1) HELIOS SYSTEMS LTD (the "Company"); and
(2) ZENITH CAPITAL LLP (the "Subscriber").

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this agreement, the following words have the following meanings:

"Subscription Price" means EUR 2,500,000.

"Claim" means any claim made by the Subscriber under this agreement.

"Bank Account" means the account notified in writing by the Company.

"Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 1.

"Claim" means any claim for breach of the Warranties.

2. SUBSCRIPTION

2.1 The Subscriber shall subscribe for the New Shares at the Subscription
Price, payable in cash.

2.2 Allotment of the New Shares shall take place at the Completion.

2.3 Any Claim shall be notified to the Company in writing.

2.4 The Warranties shall be repeated at the Completion.
