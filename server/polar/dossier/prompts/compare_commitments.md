Below are commitments taken from several documents in one transaction. Each
was quoted verbatim from the document named beside it.

Find the pairs that cannot both be honoured.

A conflict is two commitments that a single deal cannot satisfy at once: a
cap of $1.8M in one document and $2.5M in another; thirty days' notice
against sixty; governing law of England in one and New York in another; a
completion date that is before a condition's longstop.

Not a conflict:

- two documents saying the same thing in different words
- a general provision and a specific one that narrows it, where the
  document says it narrows it
- a figure that appears once — nothing to disagree with
- two commitments from the same document; that is somebody else's check
- anything you would have to assume to make disagree

Refer to commitments by their index, in brackets. Both indices must be
from the list below; anything else is dropped by a check that runs after
you.

Say what each one says. « The cap differs » is unusable; « the MSA caps
liability at $1.8M and the LOI at $2.5M » is a finding.

When they are all consistent, return an empty list. A transaction whose
documents agree is the normal case and the useful answer.
